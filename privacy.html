Simple, honest, and transparent about how we protect your information.

We collect only your email address when you voluntarily enter it into our “Stay in the Loop” newsletter form on our website. We do not collect passwords, tracking profiles, or sensitive personal data.

We use your email address exclusively to send you updates regarding new Melted chocolate flavours, seasonal treats, special offers, and news from our workshop. We keep our emails occasional and tasty — never spam.
